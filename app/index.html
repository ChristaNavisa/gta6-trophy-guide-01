    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, then the character strands, then collectibles and activities, then the combat and money cleanup. Each stage lists the trophies it earns and the order that wastes the least backtracking.

      Why this order?

      Story trophies (stage 1) unlock as you play and open the map and systems the rest of the game needs. Character strands (stage 2) are best done while the story is fresh, because their final missions are story beats. Collectibles (stage 3) and activities (stage 4) are spread across Leonida, so they share the same long drives — do them together. Combat and money trophies (stages 5–6) are the cleanup: by then you own the gear and the cash to finish them fast. The platinum pops the moment the 54th other trophy is earned.

      Missable trophies — 17 to watch

      Every trophy below is tied to a specific story chapter or mission, taken from the official descriptions. None of them is permanently missable: GTA VI lets you replay story missions, so a missed one is recovered from the mission replay menu — but finish each chapter's missions before you move on, and you will never need the replay.

        Where the facts come from

        Every trophy's name, description and grade is shown exactly as PlayStation publishes it. The official list was taken from two sources that agree on every name, description and grade:

        • Exophase — Grand Theft Auto VI trophy list
        • PSNProfiles — Grand Theft Auto VI trophy list (also the source of the PSN progress counts, e.g. 42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 meditation spots, 100 WAiNK locations)

        Each trophy's guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites those sources beside it. Where a guide says “not yet verified in play”, the advice is drawn from the official description alone — GTA VI launched on 19 November 2026 and no location or step is claimed as checked in the game.

        How the tracker works

        • Tick a trophy to mark it earned; progress is stored in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your machine.
        • Export progress downloads a JSON file; Import progress reads one back.
        • Hide story spoilers replaces the names and descriptions of un-earned story trophies with “Hidden trophy” until you earn them.
        • Filters cover story, missable, collectibles, activities, side strands, combat, exploration and odd jobs, plus grade and earned state.
        • This app is static files and works with the network off. It makes no network request at run time.
